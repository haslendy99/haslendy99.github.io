Instant Film

Take a photo. Wait for it to develop.

Instant Film turns your phone into an instant camera. Every shot is developed on film right on your phone, framed like a real print, and ready for a few handwritten words on the white border.

Five film stocks take turns, so no two prints look the same. Flash photos come out the way an instant flash lights them: a bright face against a dark room.

Your photos never leave your phone.
